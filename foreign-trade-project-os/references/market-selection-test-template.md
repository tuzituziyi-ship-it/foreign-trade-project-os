## Readiness Evidence Gate

Before outreach, verify included SKU availability, MOQ, material, finish/plating, dimensions, weight, packaging, traceable media, product cost, and provisional packed weight. Treat freight, payment cost, import/tax responsibility, compliance, and delivery as unknown until checked. Stop the market test if the offer cannot be described truthfully.

## Matched design

- Use the same compact offer and qualification rule.
- Use equal-sized finite samples of qualified buyers.
- Localize only what destination differences require.
- Record list-quality and message-quality confounders.
- Require human review before every external message.

## Strong evidence hierarchy

1. Paid order or repeat.
2. Paid sample.
3. Continued discussion after a real quote with viable economics.
4. Specific product selection and price/MOQ/freight questions.
5. Meaningful reply describing fit or objection.

Do not count opens, likes, follower counts, generic compliments, or market size as strong evidence.

## Judge gate

- Select one market only when its commercial signal is materially stronger, buyer quality is comparable, and no critical compliance/fulfillment blocker exists.
- If results are close or absent, audit list, offer, economics, readiness, and message quality; repair one dominant flaw and repeat a bounded test.
- If both look strong, still select one using evidence strength, cost to serve, compliance burden, and learning speed; move the other to Later.
